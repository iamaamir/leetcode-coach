# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Self-learners exploring algorithms and data structures for intellectual growth and enjoyment. They work independently, often in evening or weekend sessions. They value understanding over speed, and prefer to discover solutions with guidance rather than be told answers. They appreciate clean interfaces that don't distract from the problem-solving experience.

## Product Purpose

Leetcode-teacher is a web app that helps users solve LeetCode problems using local AI models. It combines AI-powered teaching with hints, structured curriculum, mock interviews, and algorithm visualization. The goal is to guide learners toward solutions without giving away answers, building genuine problem-solving skills and confidence.

## Brand Personality

Minimalist, focused, zen-like. Clean interfaces that stay out of the way of the thinking process. Technical but approachable. The aesthetic says "this is a tool for serious learners, not a gamified distraction machine."

## Anti-references

- Traditional education (generic tutorials, corporate training UIs, school-like layouts)
- SaaS landing pages (colorful cards, heavy gradients, AI-generated aesthetic)
- Crypto/Techbro (dark neon cyberpunk, aggressive color schemes)
- Gamified overload (badges, points, leaderboards that trivialize the learning)

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** - Every element earns its place. If it doesn't help the user think, it doesn't belong.
2. **Hint-first philosophy** - UI patterns should encourage exploration before revealing answers.
3. **Technical authenticity** - Lean into developer tooling aesthetics (monospace, code-centric) rather than consumer-app polish.
4. **Contextual information** - Show relevant information when needed, hide when not. Don't clutter the view.
5. **Respect the user's time** - No onboarding rabbit holes, no tutorial tooltips on every button. Let users start immediately.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Good color contrast ratios, keyboard navigable, screen reader friendly. Reduced motion support for those who prefer it.
